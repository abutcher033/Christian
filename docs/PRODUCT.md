# Christian — Product Brief

**Promise line:** Open the Book. See the story. Meet Jesus.

## Who it is for

Believers and seekers. Family-safe. Scripture-first study for anyone who wants to read the Bible, understand context and meaning, and respond in faith — not a game.

## Tone

Warm, Christ-centered, Bible-believing. We hold the authority of Scripture and the good news of grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone. We do not dunk on denominations. Clear gospel-centered wording without being corny.

## What it is

An evangelical interactive study Bible: a 3D Bible on a wooden table, real page turns through all 66 Protestant books, and tappable study entries on passages that have notes. The words on the page are the World English Bible. Notes, infographics, maps, and videos are secondary.

## Reading

- The whole Protestant canon is in the book, Genesis through Revelation.
- Navigation: book and chapter picker, reference box, swipe, tap, buttons, and keyboard.
- The last place is remembered on the device.

## Study flow (Scripture-first)

1. **Read** the verse / passage on the page
2. **Context** — who, when, where
3. **Meaning** — what the text says and teaches
4. **Response** — prayer prompt and apply

Sample notes ship for Genesis 1, John 1, and John 3. Empty states invite the reader to stay with the verse.

## Guardrails

- Do not imply baptism causes salvation
- No modalism
- Law is not left without the gospel
- No numerology or secret codes
- Do not ship NKJV, ESV, or any other copyrighted Bible text without a license
- No dark patterns, streaks, or clickbait

## Platform

Web-first 3D (Next.js + React Three Fiber). A Capacitor wrap can come later.
