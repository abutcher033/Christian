# Christian — Product Brief

**Promise line:** Open the Book. See the story. Meet Jesus.

## Who it is for

Believers and seekers. Family-safe. Scripture-first study for anyone who wants to read the Bible, understand context and meaning, and respond in faith — not a game.

## Tone

Warm, Christ-centered, Bible-believing. We hold the authority of Scripture and the good news of grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone. We do not dunk on denominations. Clear gospel-centered wording without being corny.

## What it is

An evangelical interactive study Bible: a 3D Bible on a wooden table, gesture page turns, and tappable study entries that open rich media — notes, infographics, maps, videos, timelines, and word studies. Page tap → entry list → overlay. Not just footnotes.

## Seed passages (v1)

- **Primary:** Gospel of John (1:1–18; 3:1–21; 14–17; 19–20)
- **Also:** Genesis 1–3, 12, 15, 22
- **Fixed sample pages in scaffold:** John 1, John 3, Genesis 1
- **Later (optional):** Luke 15, Romans

## Study flow (Scripture-first)

1. **Read** the verse / passage
2. **Context** — who, when, where
3. **Meaning** — what the text says and teaches
4. **Response** — prayer prompt and apply

Empty states invite the user to read the verse first before opening study media.

## Guardrails (do not)

- Imply baptism causes salvation
- Modalism
- Law without gospel
- Numerology / secret codes
- Long copyrighted Scripture without a license (see PLAN.md)
- Dark patterns, streaks, or clickbait

## Platform

Web-first 3D (Next.js + React Three Fiber). Can be wrapped later with Capacitor for mobile.
